## Evidence
`attach_annotations`: when no word precedes an annotation, the "next word"
is the next whitespace-delimited token of the remaining input, which can be
the next annotation marker itself; `last` then advances past the next
regex match and slicing panics ("byte range starts at 60 but ends at 28").

## Predicted observable failure
Two adjacent annotations before `word` at a paragraph start abort the
import. Reproduced by the reviewer.

## Approach
Matches are collected first; the look-ahead for a following word is bounded
by the next match's start, so the cursor can never pass a match.

## Files changed
- `crates/cli/src/scrivener/converter/mod.rs`

## Guard proof
`cargo test -p chikn-converter --lib -- adjacent_annotations_at_a_paragraph_start_attach_without_panic`:
two markers then `word more` → the first marks a bullet, the second wraps
`word`. With the look-ahead unbounded again the test panics; restored, it
passes.
