# s1-5: An unescaped `)` in a hyperlink target truncates the link

**Severity**: MEDIUM — recovered links point at the wrong resource and leak text into the prose
**Status**: Verified — accepted 2026-10-25 22:26 UTC

## Evidence
`crates/cli/src/scrivener/parser/rtf_text.rs` `render_field` writes the
target raw into `[text](url)`.

## Predicted observable failure
Target `https://example.com/a)b` becomes `[click](https://example.com/a)b)`;
Pandoc links to `/a` and shows `b)` as prose.

## Approach
Percent-encode the characters that break a Markdown destination (`(`, `)`,
space, `<`, `>`, and a literal `%` not already an escape) before writing
the link; `scrivcmt://<UUID>` targets are untouched by this.

- `crates/cli/src/scrivener/parser/rtf_text.rs`

## Guard proof

`https://x.y/<z>` → `<https://x.y/%3Cz%3E>`, `scrivcmt://ABC-123`
unchanged. With `markdown_destination` made an identity the test fails;
restored, it passes.
