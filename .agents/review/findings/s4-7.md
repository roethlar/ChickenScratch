## Evidence
`crates/core/src/core/compile.rs` `footnotes_to_references` takes the first
`>` after `<sup` as the end of the tag, even inside `data-body="x > y"`.

## Predicted observable failure
DOCX export omitted `x > y` from `word/footnotes.xml`. Reproduced by the
reviewer.

## Approach
The tag end is found with quote awareness: a `>` inside a quoted attribute
value does not close the tag.

## Files changed
- `crates/core/src/core/compile.rs`

## Guard proof
`cargo test -p chickenscratch-core --lib -- a_quoted_greater_than_in_the_body_keeps_the_footnote`:
bodies `x > y` (double quotes) and `a > b` (single quotes) become reference
footnotes with the escaped body. With the plain `>` search the test fails;
restored, it passes.
