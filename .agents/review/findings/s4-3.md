# s4-3: Literal inline-note text is decoded twice and loses braces and backslashes

**Severity**: MEDIUM — writer text inside an inline note is silently removed
**Status**: In progress
**Branch**: — (default-branch mode)
**Commit**: pending

Source: Reviewer (generation): codex / gpt-6-astra / xhigh / frontier (owner-named pair)

## Evidence
`crates/cli/src/scrivener/parser/rtf_prepare.rs` `lift_inline_notes`: the
literal form's body is unescaped and then parsed as RTF again, so the
writer's `{braces}` become a group and `C:\temp` a control word.

## Predicted observable failure
A literal footnote `Keep {braces} and C:\temp` imports as `Keep braces and
C:`. Reproduced by the reviewer.

## Approach
Both forms are read as RTF exactly once (no unescape step first): `\\`,
`\{` and `\}` decode to the writer's own characters, `\'xx` to a character,
and a bare control word is formatting, which is what Scrivener writes
inside the literal form.

## Files changed
- `crates/cli/src/scrivener/parser/rtf_prepare.rs`

## Guard proof
`cargo test -p chikn-converter --lib -- inline_notes_are_lifted_into_marker_fields`:
literal `Keep \{braces\} and C:\\temp` → body `Keep {braces} and C:\temp`.
With the unescape step put back the test fails; restored, it passes.
